HostName Discovery 2.1.6 - Windows x64

EXE with embedded multi-resolution icon (16 to 256 px).
The EXE and PS1 have SHA-256 Authenticode signatures from a self-signed
code-signing certificate. This does not provide public CA trust or
SmartScreen reputation. No timestamp authority was used.
The .cer contains the public certificate only. The non-exportable private
key remains in CurrentUser\My on the build machine.
No certificate was installed in trusted-root or trusted-publisher stores.

Inspect signature:
Get-AuthenticodeSignature .\HostNameDiscovery-2.1.6.exe | Format-List

Archive includes EXE, PS1, ICO, public CER, certificate metadata and this file.
SHA3-256SUMS.txt contains the consolidated SHA3-256 checksums computed
after signing. They are not SHA-256 checksums.

PS1 requires Windows PowerShell 5.1. EXE is packaged with PS2EXE.
Build script: ../../Build-SignedRelease.ps1 (requires PS2EXE and Python).
This release has not been uploaded to GitHub.
